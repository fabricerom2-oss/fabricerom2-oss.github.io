Privacy Policy

Fabrice Video Publisher · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Fabrice Video Publisher ("the tool") is a private desktop script used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels (SafeAI Watch, Océan Réel, Fabrice – Chansons du Covid Long and les comptines de fabrice). This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube API Services.

2. Data accessed

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the tool can access, for the owner's own channels only:

  • channel information (name, ID, public statistics);
  • the list of the channel's videos and playlists, and their statistics (views, likes);
  • video metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status, scheduled publication date).

The tool does not access comments, messages, subscribers' personal data, or any data from other YouTube users.

3. How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos, to set their metadata and publication dates, to organise them into playlists, and to show the owner the performance of his own videos.

4. Storage

The tool runs only on the owner's personal computer. The Google authorization token is stored locally on that computer and is never sent to any server other than Google's. No YouTube data is stored by the tool except temporary local logs and the publication schedule of the owner's own videos.

5. Sharing

The tool does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data to third parties.

6. Cookies and tracking

The tool does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking technology.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Locally stored tokens are then deleted by the owner.
